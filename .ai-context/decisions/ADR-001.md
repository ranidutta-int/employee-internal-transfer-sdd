# ADR-001: One Next.js and Node.js application

## Status

Accepted for baseline v0.2

## Date

2026-11-16

## Context

The product owner asked for a full-stack Internal Job Portal as a monolith: a Next.js experience and a Node.js service. The business baseline needs one place where duties, openings, and the decision chain run. It does not need a network of separately deployed services.

The store, service framework, styling, tests, and deployment are decided in BR-55 and TD-02 through TD-05.

## Decision

Ship one deployable application.

- Experience: Next.js, under `src/frontend/` when implementation starts.
- Service: Node.js, under `src/backend/` when implementation starts.
- Domain behaviour sits in modules listed in `.ai-context/architecture.md`.
- Workflow steps bind to duties. Industry titles are configuration labels.
- The HRMS stays the system of record for identity and for the resulting assignment.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** One journey, one audit trail, one place to enforce duties. Employers in different fields share the same modules.
- **Negative / risks:** The service and the experience change together. A later split would be a new decision, not a hidden requirement of this ADR.

## Compliance and constitution impact

- Updates `architecture.md` section: Decision in force.
- Updates `constitution.md` constraint: Architectural constraints, TD-01.
- Does not choose a database or an HTTP framework.
