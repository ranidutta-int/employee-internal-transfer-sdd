# Feature BRD: Member home and profile

**Module:** experience  
**Status:** Draft, follows baseline v0.2  
**Baseline:** `.ai-context/BRD.md` (wins on conflict)

## Objective

Each duty sees a home for the work that duty performs. A member sees their own hierarchy, skills, role, designation, and cases, and can record preferences that only narrow what policy already allows.

## Users

Member, line authority, stewards, people operations, executive observer, administrator. Each sees a different home.

## Journey

The landing step after Journey A3, and the list the member uses at the start of Journey B.

## Owned rules

BR-39, BR-40, and the presentation of facts required by BD-03.

## Owned requirements

FR-40, FR-41. Homes for other duties display work owned by mobility, opportunities, transition, and governance.

## Out of scope for this module

Editing HRMS-mastered fields. Browsing another member's profile.

## Decisions

The questions that used to block this module are closed in `.ai-context/BRD.md` section 7, baseline v0.2. The member-level outcomes are in `.ai-context/brd/end-to-end-journey.md`.
