# Feature BRD: Workforce access

**Module:** identity  
**Status:** Draft, follows baseline v0.2  
**Baseline:** `.ai-context/BRD.md` (wins on conflict)

## Objective

A person who has been onboarded in the HRMS can sign in with Google and see their employment facts. A person the HRMS has not sent cannot be added here.

## Users

Member, administrator. The HRMS is the sending system, not a human user.

## Journey

Baseline Journey A, failures FS-01 and FS-02.

## Owned rules

BR-01, BR-02, BR-03, BR-04, BR-05, BR-06.

## Owned requirements

FR-01 through FR-07.

## Out of scope for this module

Openings, approvals, transition tasks, and any local password.
