# Feature BRD: Openings and matching

**Module:** opportunities  
**Status:** Draft, follows baseline v0.2  
**Baseline:** `.ai-context/BRD.md` (wins on conflict)

## Objective

A member sees internal openings that pass their hard rules. Warn rules stay visible and travel to approvers. Frequent movement can be configured as either outcome. Nothing is ranked by a hidden score.
