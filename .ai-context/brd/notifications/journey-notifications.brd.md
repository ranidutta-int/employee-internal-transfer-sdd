# Feature BRD: Journey notifications

**Module:** notifications  
**Status:** Draft, follows baseline v0.2  
**Baseline:** `.ai-context/BRD.md` (wins on conflict)

## Objective

Each material step tells the people named in the baseline notification matrix. A failed delivery is visible and retried. It does not undo the decision.

## Users

Every duty that the matrix names. Delegates receive the notices of the duty they cover.

## Journey

The notice column of Journeys A through E. Failure FS-13.

## Owned rules

BR-35.

## Owned requirements

FR-35.

## Out of scope for this module

SMS, push, and personal mailboxes. Work email is in scope (BR-35).

## Decisions
