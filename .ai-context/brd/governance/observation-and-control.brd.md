# Feature BRD: Observation and control

**Module:** governance  
**Status:** Draft, follows baseline v0.2  
**Baseline:** `.ai-context/BRD.md` (wins on conflict)

## Objective

An executive observer can see the whole journey, stalls, and decline patterns, without that duty becoming an approver. An administrator configures policy and can unblock a stall only with a recorded intervention.

## Users

Executive observer, administrator, people operations.

## Journey

Baseline Journey E. Failure FS-15.

## Owned rules

## Owned requirements

## Out of scope for this module

Automatic removal of a steward. Automatic approval when a threshold is crossed.
